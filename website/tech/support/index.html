Mess Manager

Mess Manager questions

About the app · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

How do I join my Room?

Ask your Room Admin for the Room code. In the app, tap "Join with a code" and enter it. The Admin approves your request, and you get a notification when you are in.

Does Mess Manager send or hold money?

No. Mess Manager is a shared notebook. You pay each other the usual way, then record the payment with proof so the Admin can confirm it.

Someone changed an expense. Can I see what it was before?

Yes. Every edit and deletion stays in the expense history. Expenses can be edited only for 30 minutes after they are added, and settled months are locked.

Something in my Room looks wrong. What do I do?

Open the expense or payment and raise a flag. The Room Admin sees it and reviews it openly. If it is still not solved, write to the Grievance Officer below.

How do I remove the ads?

Choose the ad-free subscription (Monthly or Yearly) in the app. Google Play handles the payment. To cancel, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or use this link. See Plans for details.

How do I download my data?

Go to Settings in the app and choose to download your data as PDF or JSON.

How do I delete my account?

Use Settings in the app, or the delete account page if you no longer have the app. Your sign-in stops immediately. Records stay read-only for two months for your former Rooms' accounts, then everything linked to you is deleted.

Uyoon Al Wasel

Uyoon Al Wasel questions

Visit the site · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

How does someone reach me without seeing my number?

They scan the sticker on your car with their phone camera, or tap it with NFC, pick a reason such as "Lights are on" and send. You get the alert by SMS, WhatsApp or notification, in your own language. Neither side sees the other's phone number.

Does the person scanning need an app?

No. The sticker opens a web page in any phone browser, in English, Arabic, Urdu or Hindi.

How do I activate my sticker?

Sign in at wasel.utplgroup.com with your mobile number, add your vehicle and enter the activation code from the card that came with your sticker.

What happens in an emergency?

The person at your car can call the local emergency number with one tap, and alert you and up to 3 family contacts with their location. Emergencies come through even when you have paused alerts.

Can I pause alerts or stop someone who misuses my sticker?

Yes. In your dashboard you can pause alerts for a few hours, set daily quiet hours, and block a person who keeps sending unwanted alerts.

Salon Booking

Salon Booking questions

Visit the site

How do I book an appointment?

Open the booking link your salon shared with you, choose a service, a staff member and a free time, then enter your name and mobile number. You get a WhatsApp confirmation from the salon.

How do I change or cancel my booking?

Contact the salon directly on the WhatsApp number shown on its booking page. Each salon manages its own bookings.

I own a salon or nail studio. How do I get my own booking page?

Email support@utplgroup.com with your shop name and city. We set up your page with your name, logo, colour, services and staff.

Can other salons see my customers or bookings?

No. Every salon's data is kept fully separate. Only you and your staff see your bookings.
