How it works

Create a Room, add expenses, settle up

Invite members with a code and add every expense: who paid, how much and who shares it. Mess Manager works out who owes whom and suggests the fewest payments to settle.

✓Every change is recorded

Edits and deletions stay visible in the history, so nothing disappears.

✓Payments need proof

Upload a receipt and the Room Admin confirms it, so "I already paid" is easy to check.

✓Months lock after settlement

Once a month is settled, its records cannot be quietly changed.

✓Raise a flag

If something looks wrong, flag it. The Admin reviews it in the open.

✓Need Requests

Tell the Room what is needed, like gas, rice or cleaning, and see who takes it.

✓Reports you can verify

PDF and CSV reports carry a reference number anyone can check.

Free

Mess Manager Free

All features: Rooms, expenses, settlements, payment proof, reports, four languages and offline use. Small banner ads keep it free. Ads never show on payment, settlement, dispute, sign-in or account deletion screens.

Ad-free

Ad-free subscription

The same app with no ads, as a Monthly or Yearly plan. Google Play shows the price in your currency before you pay and handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose Mess Manager and tap Cancel. You keep ad-free until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription by itself.
