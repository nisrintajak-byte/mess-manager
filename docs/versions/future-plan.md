# Future plan (after v1.0 launch)

Ideas the founder has approved for later. Each becomes its own version when started.

## Children with parent/guardian consent (founder 2026-10-19)
- Today the app is 18+ only (date of birth checked at signup).
- Later update: a user under 18 enters a parent/guardian email; the parent verifies and consents
  before the account is active; parent can view the child's Rooms, revoke consent and delete the
  child's account (PRD §20.4).
- Comes with: minors cannot create a Room or be Admin; child's email never shown; only
  non-personalised, child-safe ads; Google Play Families policy; child-friendly privacy summary.
- Needs: lawyer review (India DPDP verifiable consent), email service live.

## Ad-free subscription (founder 2026-10-19)
- Users who do not want banners can pay a small subscription; ads disappear on all their devices
  (PRD §19.4 — the data model already allows it).
- Google Play Billing (required for digital purchases in Play apps); entitlement stored on the
  server per account.
- Needs: Play Console organization account + merchant/payments profile, price decision,
  Terms/Privacy update (the only payment the app itself takes).
