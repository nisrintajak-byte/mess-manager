# v0.6 — Reports, Data Retention & Account Deletion

**Status:** done — merged to main 2026-10-20 (founder OK; outside accounts still pending, see open-questions)
**Goal:** Records are exportable, kept exactly 2 calendar months, and accounts can be deleted per Google Play rules.

## In scope
- PDF & CSV reports (own / full Room for Admins), in requester's language with RTL, reference number + date
- Server-side PDF rendering with embedded Arabic, Nastaliq, Devanagari fonts
- Transactional email service (Resend or SES)
- Leaving-Room PDF statement email (§11.6)
- Scheduled jobs: 7-day pre-deletion PDF email + in-app reminder; retention deletion after 2 calendar months
- In-app account deletion flow (§18.3) incl. 2-month hold, "(account deleted)" label, final PDF
- Web deletion request page (email → confirm link)
- Data rights: download all my data, withdraw ad consent
